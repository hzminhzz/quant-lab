## Disclosure: the selection floor compounds

The DSR gate moved from `k_eff_gate` 11.6097 (after S01) to 20.1097 (after S05), with
`expected_max_sharpe_noise` 1.0567 → 1.1841. That is not the grid's doing. In
`compute_selection_report` (`src/warden/registry.py`), the applied gate is

```
k_gate = max(k_dedup, prior_max_keff) + 0.50 * unvectorized
```

and `prior_max_keff` is the previous run's *applied* gate, which already contained the
`0.50 * 17 = 8.5` penalty from the 17 unvectorized Stage-1 screens. The same 8.5 is then
added again on top of that floor, so every trial on this contract raises `k_eff` by 8.5
even though the unvectorized count never changes. Measured: 11.6097 + 8.5 = 20.1097.

This is recorded, not fixed. `src/warden/` is out of scope for this work unit, and
changing the selection formula is a contract decision for the human principal. The
practical consequence for the campaign is that each further trial on
`MULTI_ASSET_1D_V1` makes the DSR hurdle strictly harder for every later strategy.
