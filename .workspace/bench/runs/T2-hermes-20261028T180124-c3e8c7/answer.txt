Added `tests/test_carver_deadband_boundary.py` (4 tests, all passing).

What it pins, with `buffer_fraction=0.5`, `weight=1.0` → width `0.5` (exact in binary):

- `target=0.75`, `held=0.25` → drift is exactly `0.5` → held weight `0.25` is kept, and the negated mirror (`-0.75`/`-0.25`) behaves the same.
- `math.nextafter(0.75, inf)` → one representable step past the boundary → target is taken (same for the negative side).
- `target=-0.25`, `held=0.25` → equal drift on the other side of the held weight is also kept.

The module path is resolved from `__file__` rather than CWD, and a first test asserts the premise (`0.75 - 0.25 == 0.5` exactly) so a later refactor can't turn the boundary case into a rounding coincidence.
