# HYP-2026-001: Intraday Volatility-Breakout Trend Continuation on Major Crypto Perpetuals

## Economic Mechanism
In high-leverage cryptocurrency perpetual markets, sharp directional breakouts beyond normal hourly volatility (>2.0 standard deviations) trigger sequential stop-loss cascades and forced liquidations on centralized exchanges. Market makers delta-hedging short option positions exacerbate intraday momentum, producing 4 to 12 hours of autocorrelation before mean-reverting liquidity returns.

## Counterparty & Microstructure Friction
Forced liquidations must cross the bid-ask spread at market prices regardless of market impact. Slow systematic rebalancers operate on 8-hour funding intervals, allowing short-horizon trend followers to harvest continuation profits.

## Falsification Criteria
1. Stage 0 Feasibility: Holding period volatility must exceed 2.5x round-trip taker fees and slippage (12 bps).
2. Stage 1 Edge Gate: Conditional return edge $E[R \mid S \ne 0] > 1.5 \times \text{one-way cost}$ (9 bps), Profit Factor $\ge 1.15$, Time in Market $\ge 5\%$, and bootstrap $p < 0.05$.
3. Stage 2/3 Simulation: Net Sharpe $> 1.0$, 2x-Cost Stress Sharpe $> 0.50$, and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) $\ge 0.90$ across all trials.
