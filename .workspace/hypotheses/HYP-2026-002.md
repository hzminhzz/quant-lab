# HYP-2026-002: Cross-Sectional Funding-Rate & Short-Horizon Reversal Ridge & LightGBM Models

## Economic Mechanism
In cross-sectional cryptocurrency perpetual markets, extreme funding rates reflect crowded retail and directional speculative positioning. Speculators paying extreme positive (negative) funding rates incur structural carry costs that force inventory reduction, creating predictable 4-hour cross-sectional mean reversion when combined with short-horizon price exhaustion.

## Counterparty & Microstructure Friction
Aggressive retail trend followers and leveraged breakout traders continually pay 8-hour funding rates to maintain directional exposure into liquidity walls, providing systematic edge to liquidity providers taking market-neutral opposite exposure.

## Falsification Criteria
1. Stage 0 Feasibility: Holding period move volatility must exceed 2.5x round-trip cost floor.
2. Stage 1 Signal Gate: Out-of-sample Rank/Pearson IC $\ge 0.020$ with Newey-West HAC $t$-statistic $\ge 2.0$ across 4 purged walk-forward folds.
3. Linear-First Gate: Ridge linear model (`RUN-ML-001`) must establish baseline before LightGBM ablation (`RUN-ML-002`).
4. Stage 2/3 Simulation: Net Sharpe $> 1.2$, 2x-Cost Stress Sharpe $> 0.50$, and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) $\ge 0.90$.
