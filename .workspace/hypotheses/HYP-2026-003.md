# Hypothesis Brief: `HYP-2026-003` (RSI + Rising EMA + Rolling VWAP Pullback)

## 1. Source Summary & Claims Under Test
- **Source:** QuantifiedStrategies.com (*"We Combined RSI, EMA, and VWAP on QQQ - 3.54 Profit Factor"*, Oct 2, 2026, Substack Post `218001957`).
- **Reported In-Sample / Full-Sample Claims (QQQ Daily, 1999–2026, Zero Costs):**
  - Unfiltered Fast RSI only: `340 trades`, `Profit Factor = 2.19`, `Max Drawdown = 22.7%`.
  - Combined (`Fast RSI oversold` + `Longer-term EMA rising` + `Close materially below 10-bar Rolling VWAP`): `215 trades`, `Win Rate = 79.5%`, `Profit Factor = 3.54`, `Avg Return = 0.94%/trade`, `Max Drawdown = 9.1%`.
  - Sub-period decay warning noted in article: 1999–2012 (`PF = 3.73`) vs. 2018–2026 (`PF = 2.66`).

## 2. Scientific Risks to Test in `quant-lab`
1. **Selection Bias ($N$ vs. $K_{\text{eff}}$):** The authors tested *"many combinations of RSI periods, oversold levels, EMA lengths, VWAP lookback periods, and exits"* before settling on the final specification.
2. **Transaction Cost & Slippage Friction:** The published test assumed `0 bps` commissions and `0 bps` slippage.
3. **Unbounded Left-Tail Risk:** The strategy uses no hard stop-loss, relying on momentum recovery (`RSI > exit_threshold` or `Close > prior High`) to exit.

## 3. Pre-Registered 3-Trial Ablation Plan (`trials_used: 0/3`)
- **Trial 1 (`RUN-HYP003-001` — Baseline Without VWAP):** Fast RSI oversold + Rising EMA trend filter only (`vwap_discount_bps = 0.0`).
- **Trial 2 (`RUN-HYP003-002` — Full 3-Condition Filter):** Fast RSI oversold + Rising EMA trend filter + 10-bar Rolling VWAP discount (`vwap_discount_bps = 80.0` bps below 10-bar Rolling VWAP).
- **Trial 3 (`RUN-HYP003-003` — Parameter Neighborhood Perturbation):** Perturb RSI lookback/threshold and Rolling VWAP window (`10 -> 12` bars, $\pm 15\%$) to verify neighborhood stability ratio $\ge 0.70$.
