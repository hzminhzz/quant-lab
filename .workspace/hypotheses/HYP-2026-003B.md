## 4. Post-Mortem & Falsification Verdict
**STATUS: REJECTED (3-Trial Budget Exhausted on INDICES_1H_V2).**
- **Budget Accounting & Slot Usage:**
  - Slot 1 (`RUN-HYP003B-001`) was consumed when the initial backtest failed due to cross-contract return stream misalignment before `INDICES_1H_V2` was established.
  - Slot 2 (`RUN-HYP003B-002`) evaluated the clean baseline without VWAP.
  - Slot 3 (`RUN-HYP003B-003`) evaluated the full 10-bar rolling VWAP filter and bundled the $\pm 15\%$ parameter neighborhood grid directly into the run.
- **Neighborhood Stability Sign Caveat:** The reported parameter stability ratio of `1.1937` (`min_neighbor_SR / max_neighbor_SR`) is an artifact of dividing negative Sharpe numbers (e.g. $-1.15 / -0.96$). It indicates that all neighboring parameter configurations uniformly lose money, not a passing robustness metric.
- **Empirical Impact of the 10-Bar Rolling VWAP Discount Filter:**
  - **Profit Factor: SEVERELY DEGRADES.** Over the 2.5-year validation period, the baseline had Gross SR +0.72 and 1x Net Bar PF of 0.9918. Adding the 10-bar rolling VWAP discount caused Gross SR to collapse to -0.82 and 1x Bar PF to plummet to 0.7680 (and 0.7438 under 2x costs). In an hourly trending market, demanding price trade 80 bps below the rolling volume node filters out normal shallow trend pullbacks and selects for toxic left-tail momentum cascades.
  - **Max Drawdown: MIXED / FAILS AT 1x COSTS.** Under 1x costs, Max Drawdown worsened from 11.43% to 13.11%. Under 2x costs, Max Drawdown appears lower (13.95% vs. 21.53%) solely because the baseline trades 656x/year and suffers catastrophic compounding fee drag under doubled transaction frictions.
- **Conclusion:** The 10-bar rolling VWAP discount filter genuinely fails to improve out-of-sample Profit Factor on the extended Dukascopy hourly index dataset. Both baseline and filtered models fail Stage 1 and Stage 3 gates. Status: **REJECTED**.
