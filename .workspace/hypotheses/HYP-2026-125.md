## Pre-registered choices, decided before any market data was read

- **Venue substitution.** The thesis was validated on Hyperliquid; this test runs on the Binance
  UM top-100 panel (`CRYPTO_PERPS_TOP100_1H_V1`). The scoring rule is venue-neutral price
  momentum, so the substitution is testable, not assumed — but if the edge is HL-specific
  (different taker flow, different listing set) this test will not find it.
- **Universe is point-in-time by dollar volume** (`volume × close`, 7-day trailing sum). Raw
  contract volume is not used: 1000SHIB/1000LUNC-style symbols trade in millified units and would
  rank on unit inflation instead of traded value.
- **Survivorship caveat.** The panel's 100 symbols all extend to the panel end and none deleverages
  out, i.e. it is a current listing set projected backwards. Dead coins are absent, so a momentum
  long-short that would have traded a since-delisted name is invisible here. This biases the loser
  short leg optimistically. Recorded, not corrected.
- **Window.** Exploratory data ends 2025-06-30 (`val_end: 2025-07-01`). The claimed Jun-2025..Jul-2026
  window is the sealed production holdout (`holdout_start 2025-07-01`, `holdout_end 2026-10-25`) and
  was NOT read. Validation is therefore 2024-07-01..2025-07-01.
- **Label horizon.** IC is measured against a 168-bar open-to-open forward return (the actual
  holding period), not the contract's `label_horizon_bars: 4`. The contract's 4-bar purge is
  therefore shorter than the true hold; the strategy fits no parameters, so nothing is trained on
  overlapping labels, but the misalignment is disclosed rather than hidden.
- **Cost model** from `contracts/crypto_perps_top100_1h_v1.yaml`: 4.0 bps taker + 2.0 bps slippage
  per side, 1.0 bps per 8h funding, `NEXT_BAR`, `execution_delay_bars: 1`.

**Market context of the validation window:** BTC +70.2%, equal-weight panel **-25.4%**. That is a
rotation regime (BTC up, alts down) and the mirror image of the source's window (BTC -41.9%). Weekly
IC was *worse* in risk-off weeks (-0.0875, HAC t -2.28, n=20) than in risk-on weeks (-0.0178, n=31),
so a simple "they tested a bear market" explanation is not supported here.

**Liquidity check:** the selected top-25 trades a median $1.26B of 7-day quote volume (p10 $0.90B,
p90 $3.18B), so the 6 bps/side assumption is credible for these names. The panel's real defects are
current-listing survivorship (no dead coins) and venue — Binance's most-liquid 25 are the most
arbitraged names, the opposite of Hyperliquid's retail-heavy top 25.

## Code change after the T1 screen (2026-10-28)

Two defects in `strategies/candidates/xs_weekly_momentum_1h.py`, fixed at the principal's approval:

1. **Delisting / stale-price carry.** A symbol whose data ends is now force-flat from its own last
   tradable bar. Before the fix a carried weight could hold a dead name at a stale price for up to a
   week, and on the short leg that is a free survivorship credit. Coverage:
   `tests/experiments/test_xs_weekly_momentum_delisting.py` (4 cases - flat at and after the last
   bar, two-leg/gross/neutral invariant, the one-sided leg between a delisting and the next
   rebalance, and no weight surviving on a bar without a price).
2. **Window opening.** The book now also rebalances on the first bar of the validation window, not
   only on the Sunday-00:00 grid, removing 144 flat bars (1.6% of the window).

**Disclosure.** The recorded T1 row (`selection_attempts`, `kind=standalone_screen`, stage
`STAGE_1_SIGNAL`, outcome `REJECT`) was produced by the **pre-fix** module: mean IC -0.0452, HAC t
-1.01. Re-measured on the fixed module locally (unregistered diagnostic, nothing written to the
registry): mean IC -0.0481 (n 52), gross SR +0.272, net SR 1x +0.181, 2x +0.002, max DD 23.1%,
turnover 86.8x/yr, 53 rebalances. The verdict does not move: Stage 1 still rejects and 2x costs still
erase the book. No second screen was run - it would consume another selection attempt and cannot
change the conclusion.
