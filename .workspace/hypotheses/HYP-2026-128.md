## Disclosure: exploratory diagnostics preceded this registration

This is **not** a clean pre-registration. Before registering, 159 unregistered diagnostic cells were
run on the broad panel (`diag_horizon.json`, `diag_1h.json`, both persisted). Their results are the
reason this hypothesis exists and are summarised here so the reader can discount accordingly:

- rank IC of the traded direction on the 1h panel: **+0.066** at formation 168h / forward 24h, HAC
  t **+11.3** on 2021-01..2024-06, **+0.058** (t +8.2) on the 2024-07..2025-06 replication window;
- the same family's quintile books were **negative** in the mean at every configuration tried
  (SR −0.55 to −1.49 annualised, worse at 2x), because the high-score bucket's mean is dominated by a
  right tail of continued idiosyncratic pumps while its *median* is the lowest of the five buckets;
- therefore the expected outcome of this trial is a **Stage-1 pass and a Stage-3 failure**. It is
  registered to close the loop on the principal's question with harness evidence rather than a
  spreadsheet, not because the diagnostic predicts qualification.

The registered thresholds above are the sibling contract's standard gates; they were not tuned to the
diagnostic outcome. The selection count for DSR must be treated as **k >= 159 + this trial**, because
warden's `k_eff` cannot see unregistered diagnostics.

## Pre-registered choices, decided before the harness read any data

- **Sign convention in the IC series.** The Stage-1 series is the rank IC of `-score` against the
  forward label, i.e. the direction actually traded, so a positive gate means the reversal works.
  Rank (Spearman), not Pearson: the cross-section has fat tails and the skill mandates Spearman for
  cross-sectional signals. The Pearson mean is returned alongside as `mean_pearson` for audit — a
  choice that raises |IC| is disclosed, not hidden.
- **Venue substitution.** Binance UM top-100, not Hyperliquid: the lake has no HL history.
- **Survivorship.** A symbol whose data ends is force-flat from its own last tradable bar. The panel's
  symbols extend to the panel end, so this is a no-op here; it is implemented so a delisting cannot
  silently credit the short leg.
- **Double latency.** Features use bars <= t and positions are not pre-lagged; warden applies
  `execution_delay_bars` at simulation. Pre-lagging here would raise `DoubleLatencyError`.
- **Label horizon.** The contract pins `label_horizon_bars: 4`; this strategy's IC is measured over 24
  bars and its holding is 24 bars. The contract's 4-bar purge is therefore too short for the label
  actually used — recorded as `[MISSING]` rather than silently accepted.
- **`model_type: linear`, not `rules`.** There is no fitted model: the score is a linear combination of
  past residual returns, and ranking it is monotone in that combination. The harness refuses
  `cross_sectional_ml` with a non-linear `model_type` until a Ridge/linear-baseline trial exists for the
  same hypothesis; labelling this `rules` tripped that guard. `linear` describes the construction
  truthfully and *is* the linear baseline the guard is asking for — it is not a way around it.

## Trial 2 — pre-registered revision (written BEFORE the run)

Trial 1 (`RUN-XSRR-001`) passed Stage 1 (IC 0.0572, t 6.91) and failed Stage 3 on cost: net Sharpe
0.124, turnover 233.8x/yr, breakeven one-way cost 8.40 bps against 6 bps charged (1.40x margin).
The failure is a *cost* failure, so the revision targets cost and risk allocation — not the signal.
Two changes, both justified by measurements already on record, and nothing else changes:

1. **Hold 72 bars instead of 24.** The measured IC of this score is flat across the holding period
   (forward 24h −0.0660, forward 72h −0.0685 at formation 168h; HAC |t| 11.3 and 9.0). Holding three
   times longer therefore costs almost nothing in signal. Measured effect on turnover, before the run:
   **233.8x → 128.6x per year**, i.e. cost drag 14.0% → 7.7%/yr. *Not* the 3x one would guess: a 72-bar
   rebalance replaces 1.06 of the book rather than 0.64, so the realised cut is 1.8x.
2. **Weight each leg by 1/residual_vol instead of equally.** The score is already
   `sum(resid)/std(resid)` — its *ranking* is vol-normalised while an equal-weight book is not, so an
   equal-weight book over-weights the highest-vol names, which is exactly where the pump tail that
   inverts the quintile means lives (`RESULT_binance.md` §3). Weights are clipped to 0.5x..2x the equal
   weight and renormalised to 0.5 gross per leg. Measured effect on turnover: 128.6x → 133.9x, i.e.
   slightly worse, accepted because it is not a cost change but a risk-allocation one.

Expected outcome, stated before the run: **net Sharpe 0.7-1.1** — a large improvement on 0.124 and still
probably below the 1.2 bar. Anything below 1.2 closes the hypothesis at 2/3 trials; the arithmetic in
`RESULT_reversal_trial.md` says clearing 1.2 needs turnover under 7.5x/yr, and the quietest viable
rotation here is ~128x.

These two changes are combined in one trial. If the result passes, attribution requires a third trial;
if it fails, no attribution is needed. That trade-off is chosen deliberately to conserve the budget.
