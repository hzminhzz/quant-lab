# Spec: Committed dataset publication at the existing ML4T–Warden seam

> Work-unit specification — WHAT/WHY. Revised 2026-11-28 following the principal's refactor-alignment review. Supersedes the broader eight-issue proposal in this directory. Preserves the eleven DG-AC identifiers. Architecture direction approved; neither scientific authority nor release verification is implied.

## Objective

Deepen ML4T Data's existing publication interface so that it exposes coherent, immutable dataset generations consumed by Warden's existing resolver and compiler, without expanding the scientific lifecycle or introducing another authority.

## Why

Two compatibility reviews exposed one recurring defect: the producer independently mutates Parquet, metadata, quality, acceptance and catalog records, then requires callers to reconstruct consistency. Observed real-data failures include order-dependent funding qualification, an undetected renamed partition, resolution after deletion of all 83,341 BTCUSDT rows, acceptance of 175 funding rows with 93-row metadata, and acceptance of 51,869 ES one-second rows without required receipts. The first review also found stale audit qualification, readiness/identity mismatch, silent family narrowing, legacy semantics bypass and incomplete lifecycle coverage.

The recently merged Quant-Lab refactor (#151, bd222e2) removed competing research progression, duplicated preparation/finalization, state projection and private storage access. It reduced production Python by 498 lines without adding a production module, schema or dependency. Its governing principle is one owner per effect and deep existing interfaces. This work must preserve that result. It fixes the data-publication seam, not the research lifecycle.

## Ownership and invariant

- ML4T Data owns provider data, normalized partition inventory, actual data identity, source/price/instrument semantics, interval eligibility and qualification. Each family has one existing authoritative qualification policy, behind one publication interface.
- A committed dataset generation is a producer-owned publication contract binding exact immutable data and the evidence applicable to that data. It is not a new scientific subject, operation, state machine or ledger record. Unqualified data can be discovered without being research-eligible.
- The catalog indexes committed generations and their declared scope; readiness, freshness and health reports are derived observations, not authorities. An unrelated update or delayed readiness report does not invalidate another intact generation. A failed publication cannot make incomplete data eligible.
- Warden's resolver owns requirement matching and permitted metadata-only observation. Its compiler pins the selected committed generation(s), verifies the authorized input bytes and emits the existing write-once compiled contract/artifact. Once compiled, the existing contract and provenance own subsequent consumption. No producer pin propagates into research execution or scientific accounting.
- ExperimentRunner remains the scientific progression and preparation owner; ResearchRegistry owns admissions, evidence, state and recovery; EvaluationKernel owns native ML4T computation. CLI/control/jobs remain adapters. The trusted appliance owns software verification and activation, not dataset qualification.
- Core invariant: a consumer may use only a coherent committed generation for the requested eligible interval. Data, identity and required evidence never describe different generations. An incomplete update leaves the previous intact committed generation visible and usable where eligible; a genuinely damaged generation fails closed. Once compilation finishes, later producer publication cannot silently change the compiled research artifact.
- The threat model is trusted local maintenance and accidental failure, corruption, concurrency and process interruption. No additional principal authentication, signing authority or malicious-host resistance is required.

## Acceptance criteria

**DG-AC01 — One publication authority.** All active research-qualifying writer and publisher paths share the same generation invariant and one family qualification policy. Generic, specialized and scheduled publication order cannot change the verdict for identical evidence. The versioned producer contract is inspectable, portable and deterministic. Redundant qualification paths are removed, redirected or explicitly quarantined. Warden does not implement provider-specific acceptance.

**DG-AC02 — Exact identity.** A committed generation binds normalized partition membership and content digests, schema, source and logical identity, typed semantics, coverage/interval eligibility, and matching required evidence. A changed path, membership or byte content cannot retain an incompatible data identity; changed qualification/evidence cannot retain an incompatible generation identity. Equivalent republishing is idempotent. Identity excludes self-reference, absolute scratch paths and volatile freshness timestamps. Aggregate count/size/max-mtime alone is insufficient.

**DG-AC03 — Atomic visibility and recovery.** A reader of an affected dataset sees one complete prior or new committed generation, never mixed evidence. Failure or actual abrupt process termination at each durable publication boundary leaves the previous complete view, or the complete acknowledged new view, recoverable. Retry converges; no partial acceptance. Catalog indexing and derived readiness must not create separate qualification authorities. A stale or missing readiness report is explicitly observational; it cannot block an otherwise valid exact-symbol generation solely because another dataset changed. Complete-family membership is observed from a coherent catalog scope, not from a mixed series of reads.

**DG-AC04 — Incremental and concurrent operation.** Initial real-data publication and two successive updates preserve old partition bytes/rows and correct historical bounds. Independent concurrent commits do not lose references; competing updates of the same parent cannot silently overwrite each other. A reader spanning publication sees its original pinned data or refuses safely if that pin is damaged. Unlisted/staged leaves never enter a committed read. Retain old pins for the supported reader/compile and compatible recovery lifetime; do not invent permanent lake retention for already self-contained compiled artifacts.

**DG-AC05 — Correct family evidence and interval eligibility.** Metadata, quality and required acceptance bind the actual generation. Funding's authoritative validator cannot be bypassed by generic maintenance or a fresh audit of stale metadata. Dukascopy minute and second indices, Wave-1 FX, metals and other writer-owned sources retain their existing categorical per-unit evidence, source mappings, legitimate closure/source-empty classifications and price semantics. Missing receipts, SOURCE_GAP, FETCH_FAILED and unresolved units block the affected requested interval, not unrelated accepted history. Never infer acceptance from bounds, percentages or observed OHLCV. Discover unqualified data with reasons; do not force full-history qualification where existing policy supports accepted intervals.

**DG-AC06 — Bounded consumer seam.** Native ML4T query/discovery and Warden resolve/compile consume the same committed producer contract. Metadata-only resolution checks permitted inventory bindings and existence/observable identity; detect absent, renamed and detectably replaced committed leaves. Do not claim byte verification on metadata-only observation. Compiler pins exact selected generation references and authorized leaf identities; verifies the bytes actually consumed against digests after existing signed-policy, containment and time-fence checks, including change between resolution and read. Only authorized whole partitions can be read; no broad inventory hash may inspect prohibited data or straddling partitions. Refusal leaves no partial compiled artifact, contract or scientific effect. Successful output uses existing compiled artifact/contract identity and provenance. Already valid signed or compiled contracts do not depend on current producer generation, readiness, or a newer unrelated publication. No live-generation pin in ExperimentRunner, ResearchRegistry, EvaluationKernel, admission, job control or the appliance. Legacy mutable sources cannot authorize new compilation by fallback.

**DG-AC07 — Semantic and diagnostic correctness.** Match provider, instrument, cadence, price basis and required history without substitution. Positive real-data OHLCV reads/resolution for EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY and XAUUSD/XAGUSD where evidence supports them; bid-only FX is not executable bid/ask, metals are proxies, CFDs are not exchange futures, GC/SI continuous archives are not maturities or verified rolls. Native flat archives are discovered using actual authoritative files and provenance; report limitations rather than force Hive or scientific qualification. Distinguish absence, incomplete publication, missing evidence, corruption, operational staleness, insufficient history, unsupported semantics, incompatible basis and missing scientific policy. Complete-family requests cannot silently discard untyped/unqualified members; current inventory does not establish historical PIT membership.

**DG-AC08 — Real-interface adversarial proof.** A deterministic harness exercises actual producer and consumer revisions, native public interfaces and disposable copies of real authorized exploratory Parquet. Assert positive publication, native read, Warden resolution and one real native fenced compilation using isolated test-scoped authority; prove missing real policy refuses before price reads. Inject failure/process termination at durable boundaries, retry/reopen, concurrent same/different-dataset publishers and readers, stale evidence, deletion, rename, replacement (including same-size/restored timestamps), extra leaves, publisher order, missing minute/second receipts, incomplete families and legacy bypass. Include an unrelated publication/readiness update that must not invalidate the pinned eligible input. Verify no partial outputs/effects on refusal. No mocked availability or negative-only recovery success. Fault synchronization is deterministic, not sleep-based. All prior P1 findings receive public-interface witnesses.

**DG-AC09 — Safe adoption.** Migrate current stores from actual authorized bytes and existing evidence, retain original receipts, preserve keys/aliases, approved family scope, native loaders, prior signed artifact/contract/trial identities and compatible recovery. Active paths either participate in the invariant or are explicitly quarantined/disabled with owner and reason. No silent legacy downgrade, destructive overwrite, invented receipts, full-lake mirror or mandatory redownload. Rehearse on independent disposable data and preserve unrelated work. Previously valid immutable research artifacts remain usable without the mutable lake.

**DG-AC10 — Bounded resources and simpler ownership.** Reuse the existing Parquet, native provider, validation and maintenance interfaces; changed-data work is bounded by the actual affected inputs and necessary evidence, not whole-lake re-copy/rehash or unrelated audits. Measure identical-input baseline/candidate initial adoption, unchanged republish, two tails, discovery/resolution and authorized compile; report elapsed time, read/hashed/copied bytes, peak memory/disk and applicable service budgets. The earlier 1.15 s versus 119.14 s catalog timings are uncontrolled context only. Preserve fresh binding/byte verification where needed; no stat-only integrity cache. Apply the deletion test: remove obsolete duplicated qualification and coordination rather than wrapping it. No line-count target that removes essential behavior.

**DG-AC11 — Refactor and release preservation.** The existing research progression, scientific preparation, evidence/registry schemas, scientific state machines, budget/selection accounting, ML4T execution and statistical gates remain unchanged. No additional service, scheduler, database, research runner, authority or generic framework. Verify the pinned research lifecycle reference (tests/fixtures/research_lifecycle_d1e9325.json and tests/test_research_lifecycle.py), native provenance/fence regressions and unchanged strict performance witnesses. Document changed production modules/dependencies and any justified minimal seam expansion; do not reorganize unrelated owners. Both full applicable producer and exact immutable consumer release gates must pass, including legitimate resolution of pre-existing producer baseline failures. Review both standards and spec against final exact pair; compatible cutover, actual scheduled producer-to-consumer operation, health and recovery are observed before declaring engineering acceptance. Scientific readiness is reported separately: replay the six frozen bulk-intake requirements and reassess fourteen tracks after integration; recover ordinary data blockers, advance only existing-authority eligible operations. Missing policy, intent, conformance or entitlement is not a failure of engineering publication and cannot be bypassed to meet a research count.

## In scope

- Existing ML4T Data ingestion/publication/cutover seam, family policies, catalog and derived observations; native loader compatibility.
- Existing Warden resolve/compile and input provenance interfaces only, plus relevant tests and narrow documentation. Preserve the existing artifact and research interfaces.
- Real-data cross-repository invariant test, active-writer consolidation, safe adoption, resource measurement, full verification and compatible cutover. Reconcile draft PRs #48/#152 and previous evidence.
- Separate research readiness follow-up using the existing Warden workflow; no new scientific admissions or outcomes demanded by engineering acceptance.

## Out of scope

- New storage platform, full-lake mirror, additional service/DB, research ledger, orchestration layer, generic framework, signing infrastructure or alternate research path.
- Refactoring ExperimentRunner, ResearchRegistry, EvaluationKernel, CLI/control/jobs or appliance merely to accommodate publication. No new scientific subjects, state machine, schema or lifecycle.
- Changing thresholds, policy, selection debt, statistical/economic semantics, historical receipts, or trading authority. No unapproved scoring, independent-conformance invention, sealed holdout access, live orders or purchases.
- Mandatory all-history or all-track qualification; forced source substitution; speculative garbage collection.

## Constraints

- The single-owner trusted research appliance ADR and merged PR #151 are architectural constraints, not inspiration for another appliance. Preserve their metadata-only vs verified-read distinction.
- Producer owns evidence and interval policy; Warden owns semantic requirements, signed venue policy, fences and authorized effects. Readiness is never a grant.
- No sealed holdout enumeration, stat, hash, sample or read. No canonical registry, exploratory data or cache symlinks in verification; use bounded independent disposable copies. Respect exact source paths and existing time fences.
- Preserve unrelated dirty/untracked work; no destructive Git operations. Verify exact compatible revisions and release receipts; no activation on a failed gate.
- Publication identity must be portable and stable. Reuse unchanged immutable data safely; never share mutable backing bytes as a stable pin. Retain reachable generations and rollback until verified safe; no new permanent retention obligation for self-contained artifacts.
- No partial accepted publication or research effect on failure. Test actual crash/reopen semantics on the real filesystem; distinguish observed process-crash recovery from unsupported power-loss guarantees.
- Keep documentation and agent workflow bounded: one owner, one invocation pattern, one public test surface. No manual per-stage evidence assembly by Warden.

## Existing surface (must not break)

- Existing native dataset keys, aliases, providers, price/cadence semantics, Hive layouts or explicit versioned migration, maintained schedules, stage budgets and producer acceptance obligations.
- Catalog/readiness diagnostic clients and native readers, using coherent committed references or explicit version refusal, not implicit downgrade.
- DataRequirement serialized identity when optional fields are absent; resolve decision vocabulary; exact signed-contract reuse; compiled contract format/provenance compatibility and fences.
- Research progression and golden witness, ExperimentRunner, ResearchRegistry, EvaluationKernel, scientific policies, selection debt, historical artifacts, exact request/receipt recovery and existing immutable release verifier.

## Open questions

- Producer owns the minimal physical layout, identity serialization and commit/reader coordination mechanism. Choose from actual filesystem, native query and existing cutover constraints; prove a real vertical slice before scaling. Do not preselect a new storage abstraction.
- Determine safe pin lifetime and retention from actual readers, compile operations and compatible recovery targets. No deletion without proven reachability; self-contained compiled artifacts require no live lake pin.
- Choose adoption batches from actual evidence and active-writer inventory; unresolved source gaps remain unqualified at their actual interval scope. Existing producer policy owns that judgment.
- Baseline producer failures and concurrent work: identify actual owner and resolve without weakening verification; ordinary engineering blockers are within execution scope.
- Scientific and entitlement prerequisites are separate existing authority decisions. Report them and continue eligible work without changing policy or fabricating acceptance.
